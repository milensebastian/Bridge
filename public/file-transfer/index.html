Bridge file transfer between your phone and laptop

Move a photo, PDF, video or folder between two devices without a cable, app or account. Bridge uses a QR code to pair the browsers and keeps the transfer between your devices.

Open Bridge file transfer

How Bridge file transfer works

  1. Open Bridge on your laptop and select Create a connection.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone and compare the four symbols.
  3. Open Files on either device, choose your files, and save them on the other device.

What can you send?

Send photos, videos, PDFs, documents, ZIP files, text files and folders. Folders are delivered as a ZIP with their relative paths preserved. Clipboard text and links are available in the Clipboard tab.

Is Bridge file transfer free?

Yes. The browser app is free to use and does not require an account. Direct transfers work best. A free relay is available for networks that cannot connect directly and has a limited monthly allowance.

Bridge file transfer is private

Files are encrypted in the paired browser session. The signaling service helps the browsers find each other and does not act as a file library.
