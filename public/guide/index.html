bridge

Send a file from your phone to your laptop

Have a photo or PDF on your phone and need it on your computer? Open Bridge on your laptop and keep your phone handy. Both devices use their browsers.

Open Bridge

Pair the two screens

  1. On the laptop, choose Create a connection. Leave the QR code on screen.
  2. Scan it with your phone camera and open the link. Compare the four symbols on both screens, then select The symbols match on each device.

Send the file and save it

  1. On the phone, open Files, tap Choose files, and select your photo or PDF.
  2. Keep both tabs open and the phone awake until the transfer finishes.
  3. Check the laptop's downloads. If you need to save it again, choose Save file in the transfer queue. Folders arrive as a ZIP with their paths preserved.
Watch the three-step transfer demo

Can I send files the other way?

Yes. Choose the files on the laptop and save them on the phone. You can also drag a folder into the laptop's Files tab.

Do both devices need the same Wi-Fi?

Bridge tries a direct encrypted connection over the internet and can use a relay when the networks require it. Relay capacity is limited. If pairing expires, create a fresh connection on the laptop.

What else can I do once paired?

Use Clipboard to send a link or a note. In Touch & sign, open a PDF on the laptop and use the phone to advance slides or draw a signature.
